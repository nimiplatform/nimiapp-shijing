# ShiJing User Guide

ShiJing pairs deterministic classical Four Pillars (Bazi), 24 Solar Terms, and major-cycle calculations with AI-assisted semantic reflection, helping you observe personal rhythms, clarify decisions, and understand relationships over time.

---

## Core Features & Getting Started

### 1. Birth Profile & Chart Calculation
- Enter your birth date, time, and location. If your exact birth hour is unknown, check **Unknown birth time**.
- Incomplete profiles can be saved at any time. Opening a complete Natal Mirror reading requires birth time, the gender setting required by the active calculation method, and at least one active focus topic. The app explains any missing inputs and never fabricates a birth hour.

### 2. Explore the Six Mirrors
- **Daily Mirror (RiJing)**: Log daily reflections and key plans alongside the day's stem-branch climate and traditional calendar cues.
- **Monthly Mirror (YueJing)**: View the next 30 days of five-element shifts to pace important decisions and windows of effort.
- **Yearly Mirror (NianJing)**: Observe annual transitions and multi-year turning points across your chosen focus areas.
- **Natal Mirror (MingJing)**: Read your personal natal chart interpretation covering natural strengths, tendencies, and recurring patterns.
- **Relationship Mirror (HeJing)**: Add family members, partners, or collaborators to examine shared dynamics, strengths, and friction points.
- **Inquiry Mirror (WenJing)**: Hold multi-turn AI conversations about real-life questions grounded in your chart context, with explicit notes on uncertainty.

### 3. Navigation & Inquiry Sidebar
- Use Back and Forward navigation to return to previously visited views without triggering duplicate AI readings.
- Among the six mirrors, only **Inquiry Mirror** includes a collapsible sidebar; collapsing it preserves your conversation history.

## Privacy & Data Handling

- **Stored on Your PC**: Birth details, calculated charts, and private consultation logs are stored in Nimi App private storage on your host PC. AI interpretations follow your configured Local or Cloud model route; when using a Cloud route, only the chart and question context needed for that turn is sent to your chosen provider.
- **Nimi Account Access**: The desktop app follows your Nimi Home session. Admitted Web and Android preview entries sign in through the official Nimi account page and require first-time source confirmation in Nimi Home on your primary PC. Keep Nimi Home and Runtime online and reachable when using Web or mobile; closing a browser tab does not stop Runtime. The Web edition can be opened from the [Nimi App Storefront](https://nimi.ai/apps/shijing) or [shijing.nimi.xyz](https://shijing.nimi.xyz/).
- **Saving & Reconnecting**: Disconnecting or signing out closes the active private view. Reconnecting reloads saved archives; in-flight streams and unsaved drafts are not auto-recovered. Edit a given archive from one client at a time, as concurrent edits across surfaces are not automatically merged.
- **Full Deletion Control**: Every locally saved profile, log, and conversation can be permanently deleted at any time.
